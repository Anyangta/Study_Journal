## 4. 초록 초안 (구조 템플릿)

> **주의**: 아래 수치는 재측정 대상입니다.
> m6·m8 완료 후 `../ICNGC_2차논문/verify.py --update` 값으로 교체하세요.
> `[N]` 표시된 곳이 갱신 대상입니다.

```
Stream processors with exactly-once state must checkpoint, and the interval is
left to the operator. The advice in circulation is the classical batch rule,
tau* = sqrt(2 delta M), which assumes a failure destroys the work since the last
checkpoint and that the work is redone at the rate it was first done. Fed by a
durable log, neither assumption holds: the source rewinds and the records are
read again, and that replay competes with live arrivals for the same capacity.
The cost of a failure is therefore the area under the backlog it leaves —
quadratic in the checkpoint age where the batch cost is linear — and the optimum
grows more slowly than the square root, with a local exponent of [0.43] falling
to [0.35] over the practical range.

We derive the latency-optimal interval and a stability bound that the classical
rule cannot express, and measure every term on a Flink/Kafka deployment. Two
measurements move the answer more than the exponent does: a checkpoint delays
[5.8x] more record-seconds than the capacity it consumes, and the outage a record
experiences is [4.3 s] where the engine reports [1.2 s]. The failure term is
predicted from per-episode measurements with no free parameter (R2 = [0.999]).
The two rules separate by [2.2x] at an MTBF of one hour and [3.5x] at one day.
```

**약 190 단어.** 5요소가 다 있고 수치가 4개 들어갑니다.

### 이 초안에서 잘한 것 (따라 하실 때 참고)

- **첫 문장이 문제**입니다. 배경이 아니라.
- **두 번째 문장에 기존 답과 그 가정**이 나옵니다. 심사자가 여기서 "아, 그
  가정이 깨지는구나"를 예상하게 됩니다.
- **"neither assumption holds"** — 반박이 명확합니다.
- 결과 수치가 **네 개** 들어가고, 각각 다른 것을 말합니다
  (지수, 배율 2개, R², 격차).
- 마지막 문장이 **"그래서 얼마나 차이나는가"** 입니다.
