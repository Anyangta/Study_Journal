## Title

**Checkpoint Intervals for Latency-Sensitive Distributed Stream Processing**

(대안: *Optimizing Checkpoint Intervals for Record Latency, Not Utilization, in Distributed Stream Processing*)

---

## Abstract (150~200 words)

Distributed stream processors checkpoint periodically to recover exactly-once
state after failure, and the interval must be chosen. Prior work on
stream-specific checkpoint intervals optimizes **system utilization** — the
fraction of time spent on useful work [REF: Jayasekara]. We argue that for
latency-sensitive pipelines the relevant objective is different: the
**record-weighted end-to-end latency** that an SLA actually measures. Under a
durable source, a failure does not destroy work but rewinds the source, and the
replayed records must drain through the spare capacity left after serving live
input. This makes the failure cost an **area of accumulated backlog, quadratic in
the checkpoint age**, whereas the utilization cost is linear. We derive the
latency-optimal interval, show it grows more slowly in the mean time between
failures than the utilization optimum, and measure every term on Apache Flink 1.20
with a Kafka source across 255 single-node runs and a two-node distributed
configuration. The per-episode failure cost is predicted from measured quantities
alone at R²=0.996. We also report a practical estimate — the equivalent stall is
roughly half a checkpoint's duration on local storage — and, in the two-node case,
that distributed recovery doubles the restore time.

## 1. Introduction (약 0.75쪽)

**문단 1 — 문제.**
> A distributed stream processor with exactly-once semantics must checkpoint its
> operator state periodically, and an operator must decide how often. Too frequent,
> and checkpointing steals capacity from live processing; too rare, and a failure
> forces a long catch-up.

**문단 2 — 기존 답과 그 목적함수.**
> The classical checkpoint-interval result, τ*=√(2δM), balances checkpoint cost
> against lost work and comes from batch/HPC computing [REF: Young][REF: Daly].
> Stream-specific refinements exist: they optimize **utilization** and find the
> optimum to depend only on checkpoint cost and failure rate [REF: Jayasekara],
> or assign per-operator intervals [REF: Zhuang], or model tuple latency under
> workload intensity [REF: Zhang].

> ⚠️ **여기서 "아무도 안 했다"고 쓰면 안 됨 (거짓).** 위처럼 선행연구를 인정하고,
> 아래 문단 3에서 우리 차별점을 좁게 제시.

**문단 3 — 우리 차별점.**
> These works optimize utilization or throughput. We instead optimize the
> **record-weighted latency** an SLA measures, and we show the two objectives
> diverge because the underlying cost has a different shape. With a durable log,
> a failure rewinds the source rather than destroying work, and the replayed
> records compete with continuously arriving input for spare capacity. The
> backlog this creates, and the latency area under it, is **quadratic** in the
> checkpoint age — not linear as in the utilization/lost-work accounting.

**문단 4 — 기여.**
> We contribute: (i) a record-latency model of checkpoint-interval selection under
> durable replay, and its optimum; (ii) a measurement of every term on Flink/Kafka,
> single-node (255 runs) and two-node; (iii) a parameter-free validation of the
> quadratic failure cost (R²=0.996); (iv) a practical stall estimate (δ≈d/2 on
> local storage) and its limits under distribution.

## 2. Background and Related Work (약 0.75쪽) — Regular에서 새로 확장

**2.1 Checkpointing in batch vs. streaming.**
- 체크포인트의 기원(HPC), Young/Daly. 배치는 되감기 없음.
- 스트리밍(Flink ABS): 상태 + 소스 오프셋(책갈피)을 함께 저장 → 되감기 가능.

**2.2 Stream-specific checkpoint interval work.**
| 연구 | 목적함수 | 우리와의 차이 |
|---|---|---|
| Jayasekara [REF] | utilization 최대화 | 우리는 record latency |
| Zhuang [REF] | operator별 간격 | 우리는 전역 latency 최적 |
| Zhang [REF] | tuple latency + recovery | 우리는 durable replay의 backlog 기하 명시 |

> **정직하게**: 이들도 latency를 부분적으로 다룸. 우리 novelty는
> **record-weighted latency를 목적함수로 명시 + 되감기 backlog 기하의 quadratic
> 구조를 유도·실측**한 점. 원문 읽고 이 문장 정밀화 필요.

## 3. Model (약 1쪽)

**3.1 Setup.**
λ 도착률, μ 서비스율, ρ=λ/μ, τ 주기, a 장애 시 마지막 체크포인트 경과,
D 실효 공백, μ_d 복구 중 처리율.

**3.2 Failure cost — 삼각형 기하 (논문의 심장).**
> After restart the oldest surviving record is (a+D) old. Backlog B₀=λ(a+D)
> drains at rate (μ_d−λ), so catch-up takes T_c=λ(a+D)/(μ_d−λ). Latency falls
> linearly from (a+D) to 0, so the latency-time area is a triangle; since records
> emerge at μ_d during drain:

```
A = μ_d · λ · (a+D)² / (2(μ_d − λ))
```

> **이 유도는 외부감사에서 재유도해도 맞음 (통과).**

**3.3 Optimal interval.**
장애가 스케줄과 독립이면 a~U(0,τ), E[(a+D)²]=τ²/3+τD+D².

```
Lbar(τ) = ℓ₀ + δ²/(2(1−ρ)τ) + μ_d·E[(a+D)²]/(2(μ_d−λ)M)
```

미분하여 τ*를 얻음. D≪τ에서 τ*∝(δ²M)^(1/3), D≫τ에서 τ*∝δ√(M/D).

**3.4 Utilization optimum, for contrast.**
용량 회계 시 τ*_util=√(2δM/ρ). 두 목적함수의 답이 M에서 다르게 스케일.

**3.5 Mean-load stability condition (hard bound 아님!).**
```
λ(τ/2+D)/(μ_d−λ) + D < M   (first-moment stability)
```
> ⚠️ **"hard bound"로 쓰지 말 것.** 장애가 stochastic이므로 평균 부하 기준
> 근사 조건. τ=64 안정/128 발산은 관측 증거이나 deterministic 증명 아님.

## 4. Experimental Setup (약 0.75쪽)

**4.1 Cluster.** i9-9900K 16스레드 노드. 단일노드 255런 + 2노드(cluster03 복구).
Flink 1.20.5, Kafka 3.9.2 KRaft, RocksDB 풀 체크포인트.

**4.2 Workload.** 합성 생성기(상태 크기·CPU 독립 조절), 레코드에 발신 시각.
계측은 잡 내부 500ms 지연 요약(엔진 메트릭은 60초 평균이라 미사용).

**4.3 Failure injection — 정확한 표현.**
> Failures are generated by a **renewal process with exponential gaps truncated
> below at 40 s** (to keep episodes independent). The nominal MTBF of 55 s yields
> a **measured mean gap of 72.8 s** (median 40 s); we use the measured inter-kill
> times, not the nominal value, wherever M enters.

> ⚠️ **"Poisson MTBF=55s"로 쓰면 부정확** (외부감사 2번). 위처럼 정확히.

**4.4 Analysis pipeline + exclusions.**
- analyze→drift→model→verify→plot (코드 전부 공개, verify.py로 재검증).
- 제외: saturated(τ<2d), unstable, overloaded, censored. **각 기준 명시.**
- **2d는 물리 하한 아니라 회귀 유효성을 위한 empirical safety threshold**로 정의.

## 5. Results (약 1.75쪽)

**5.1 The optimum exists and moves with M.**
정상 지연이 1/τ로 감소(fig1), 내부 최적점 존재.

**5.2 Parameter-free failure-cost validation (핵심 증거).**
```
A_pred = μ_d·λ·peak²/(2(μ_d−λ))   (측정값만, 자유 파라미터 0)
```
| 조건 | 에피소드 | R² | 기울기/예측 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100MB ρ0.5 | 30 | **0.996** | 1.107 |
| 200MB ρ0.3 | 61 | **0.982** | 1.092 |

**5.3 Two checkpoint costs (currency mismatch).**
용량 δ_cap=0.15~0.22s vs 지연 δ=1.3~4.2s → **8.8~18.6배.**
δ가 제곱으로 들어가 utilization 기준 값은 latency 목적함수에서 크게 과소평가.

**5.4 The δ≈d/2 estimate — and its limit.**
| 조건 | δ/d |
|---|---|
| 1노드 (4조건) | **0.43~0.61** (ρ버그 수정 후) |
| 2노드 | **0.18~0.23** |

> ⚠️ **일반 법칙으로 주장 금지 (외부감사 6번).** "1노드 로컬 저장소에서의
> 경험적 추정"으로 범위 한정. 2노드가 이 비율을 깸.

**5.5 Distance to the classical/utilization optimum.**
| M | latency-optimal | utilization-optimal | 배율 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 55s | 3.6s | 7.0s | 1.9× |
| 1h | 18s | 56s | 3.1× |
| 1d | 56s | 275s | 4.9× |

**5.6 Storage drift as a methodology result (fig5).**
31시간 288→63 MB/s. 조건 섞으면 area R² 0.98→0.02. → **순서 무작위화·대조런 필수.**

**5.7 Mean-load stability ceiling.**
τ=64 안정, τ=128 발산. 예측 한계 부근. 큰 상태는 무장애에도 못 따라감.

**5.8 Distributed (two-node) recovery ⭐ — 새 절.**
> On two nodes with remote (MinIO/S3) checkpoint storage, a failed operator's
> state is restored across the network. **Engine-reported restore time rose from
> 1.2 s to 2.34 s** (7 episodes). δ, in contrast, fell (checkpoint writes spread
> across two disks). Thus distribution raises D but lowers δ — opposite effects on
> the optimum.

> ⚠️ **인과 표현 주의 (외부감사 7번)**: "network distance 때문에"라고 단정 금지.
> 저장소 백엔드도 동시에 바뀜(로컬→MinIO). "distributed + remote-storage
> configuration에서 restore time 증가"까지만.

## 6. Limitations (약 0.5쪽) — 정직하게

- **Two confounded changes** in 2-node (topology + storage backend).
- **Clock skew** ~0.1 s between nodes; sub-second 2-node δ comparisons are
  therefore imprecise; we rely on the larger D effect.
- **δ≈d/2** is an empirical estimate for local storage, not a law.
- **Stability condition** is a mean-load approximation, not a hard boundary.
- 34% of runs usable (storage drift); reported, not hidden.
- Single failure mode (kill -9), synthetic workload.

---

## 7. Conclusion (약 0.3쪽)

> Checkpoint-interval selection in stream processing has been framed around
> utilization. For latency-sensitive pipelines the objective differs, and so does
> the answer: durable replay makes the failure cost quadratic in the checkpoint
> age, pushing the latency optimum below the utilization optimum by up to 5× at a
> daily failure rate. We validated the quadratic cost with no free parameter
> (R²=0.996) and characterized how distribution raises recovery cost. A checkpoint
> costs, in latency terms, roughly half its wall-clock duration on local storage —
> a starting point practitioners can use without a sweep.
